Write {{target}} in the fewest Classical Chinese characters that still carry
every fact.

- Classical, not modern Mandarin. No 的, no 了, no 是 as a copula, no 我們.
- Two to four characters per line. One fact per line. No function words at all
  unless the line is false without them.
- Omit every subject, pronoun and final particle.
- "->" means 則, "=" means 為, "!" marks a warning. Use the symbols, not the
  characters, because they cost less.

Keep every identifier, number, path, flag and error string in its original
script, exactly as it is, capitalisation included. Never transliterate a symbol
name into Chinese, and never translate a literal string the program contains.

Never drop a fact to hit the length. Answer at whatever length the facts need,
and spend nothing on anything else. A fact you cannot say in four characters is
a fact you say in more.
