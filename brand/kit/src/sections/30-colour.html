Colour

True neutral greys, like your screenshot, with no tint. One accent: Demido green, unless you or your system choose another. Four signals that appear only when true. When nothing needs you, the only colour on screen is the model that is awake.

Demido green

Demido's colour is green. In the interface it means one thing: alive. The model that is awake wears it, keyboard focus wears it, and good news wears it. It is soft enough to sit beside your mark, clearly green rather than teal, and it stays apart from caution, danger and info, including for red-green colour blindness.

The accent is a setting

Green is the default, not a rule. If your system has an accent colour, Demido follows it, wherever that colour comes from. You can also pick any colour. Change it here and the whole page follows.

Appearance

Accent colour Changed Uses your system's accent colour when it has one. Otherwise Demido green.
Details
Default Automatic. Automatic follows the system accent whenever the system reports one, whether it comes from your wallpaper or was set by hand. With no system accent it is Demido green. Whichever colour comes in, Demido keeps its hue and strength and holds its lightness in a band that reads on the current theme, so a very dark or very pale accent still shows. Signals

Each one is a glyph, a word and a colour together. The colours were chosen to stay apart from each other and from the accent, including for red-green colour blindness. Signal colours are for a glyph and a short label, never for running text.

Awake Success Caution Danger Info
Awake Success Caution Danger Info
NOTE
Success is Demido green, always, even when the accent is another colour. With the default accent, "awake" and "success" share the green on purpose: both are good news, and their glyphs differ (an eye, a check).
NOTE
Measured for red-green colour blindness: on dark, green stays 11 or more away from caution, danger and info (our target is 8). On light, green and caution come to 7.8, just under target; the glyph shape tells them apart.
CAUTION
A custom or system accent can land on a signal colour: a red accent looks like danger, an amber one like caution. Demido never relies on colour alone, so nothing breaks, but the glyph and the word then carry the meaning.
NOTE
On light, danger is a deep crimson on purpose. A brighter red collapses into caution for people with red-green colour blindness.
