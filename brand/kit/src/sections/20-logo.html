Two cuts Proposal

Your master is drawn for large sizes. Below about 40 px its strokes fall under 2 px and the eyes blur. The small cut keeps the same drawing with heavier strokes (ring 24, stems 26) and eyes 12% larger. Both rows below are real pixels.

App icon and tray

The app icon is the white mark on a near-black tile, nothing else. Sizes of 48 px and below use the small cut and a larger mark.

Awake: a model is loaded Asleep: nothing loaded

Proposal The tray icon tells you the state without opening the app: eyes open when a model is in memory, closed when none is. It changes your mark in one place only, so it is yours to accept or drop.

Notes for your review

NOTE
Below about 24 px the mark can read as a yin-yang symbol. The small cut makes it clearer but does not remove that reading.
NOTE
The masters keep strokes as strokes, as in your file. For print, cutting or embroidery, outline them in Illustrator from Logo.ai. Your source file also has one stray 1 pt stroke on the upper eye; the masters leave it out.
CAUTION
The name and the mark have not been checked against trademark registers. The repo is GPL, but a logo usually should not be: decide how others may use the name and mark before the repo goes public.
